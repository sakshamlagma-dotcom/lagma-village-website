About the Developer

Saksham Kumar Jha created the Lagma Village Official Website as a digital place for village information, public services, local notices, gallery content, and useful online resources.

I focus on building accessible, lightweight, and user-friendly web experiences that help communities find information quickly and clearly. The website brings together village identity, local resources, and public service details in one polished platform.

The project is built around the Lagma Village community and is designed to remain simple, fast, and useful across desktop, mobile, and tablet devices.
